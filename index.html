🧭 Guidelines for High-Accuracy Leaf Diagnosis

  1. Focus on a single leaf: Position the leaf flat and central in the camera frame. Multiple leaves or busy backgrounds can confuse the model.
  2. Ensure adequate lighting: Avoid dark shadows or strong backlighting. Natural daylight or a bright indoor lamp works best.
  3. Capture texture details: Hold the camera stable and tap the screen to focus. Avoid blurry, out-of-focus, or shaking images.
  4. Avoid extreme conditions: Extremely wet leaves or heavily withered parts may alter color representations. Choose representative spots.
