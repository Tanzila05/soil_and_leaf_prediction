🧭 Guidelines for High-Accuracy Soil Prediction

  • Clear Lighting: Take the photo under natural sunlight or bright, direct indoor lighting. Avoid shadows.
  • Dry/Consistent State: Ensure the soil sample is representative. Extremes of mud or dry dust may affect color prediction.
  • Focus & Close-up: Position the camera 6 to 12 inches directly above the soil surface so the texture is clearly visible.
  • Clean Sample: Clear away loose leaves, large stones, twigs, or weeds from the frame before snapping the photo.
